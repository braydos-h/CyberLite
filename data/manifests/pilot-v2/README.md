## Limitations

- The 13-gram screen catches verbatim and near-verbatim overlap only. Paraphrase, translation, and conceptual contamination are not detected.
- Manual review is pending; quality gates are heuristics, not ground truth.
- Upstream documentation can be outdated or incorrect. Records preserve upstream claims without independently verifying them.
