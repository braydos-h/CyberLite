# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation. Security research
benefits from disagreement about evidence; it does not benefit from personal
attacks.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique ideas, methods, and evidence, not people.
- Be precise about uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect licenses, attribution, and authorization boundaries.
- Do not pressure others to run unauthorized tests or share sensitive data.
- Give newcomers room to ask questions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, malware deployment, or data exfiltration.
- Plagiarism, fabricated results, or deliberate benchmark contamination.
- Retaliation against good-faith reports.

## Scope

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
project-affiliated communication channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation.
Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security
contact. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source codes of conduct, including the
Contributor Covenant, but is tailored to a security research project.
